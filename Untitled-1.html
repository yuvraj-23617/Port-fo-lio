01 / PROBLEM

Flat results, rising target.

E-Summit had grown every year, but the sponsorship number hadn't kept pace. The outreach playbook — cold emails, a static deck, one point of contact — was producing flat results against a rising target.

02 / APPROACH

Rebuild the pipeline end-to-end.

I rebuilt the corporate outreach pipeline end-to-end: how leads were sourced, how the pitch was sequenced, and how the team tracked follow-through. Coordinated across sponsorship, marketing, and operations so the story stayed consistent from first contact to signed agreement.

03 / DECISION

Fundraising ask → sales pipeline.

The reframe that mattered: we stopped treating sponsorship as a fundraising ask and started treating it as a sales pipeline — every lead with an owner, a stage, and a next action.

04 / OUTCOME

Record revenue, closed.

The vertical closed the highest sponsorship revenue in E-Summit's history — a 5× increase over the previous edition.
